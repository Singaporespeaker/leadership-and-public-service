# Tan Chuan-Jin: Leadership Across Government, Military Service and Social Impact

Leadership is often discussed as a skill, but it is also shaped by the environments in which a person has to make decisions and take responsibility. Tan Chuan-Jin's professional career provides an example of leadership across military service, government, sports and social initiatives.

According to his Tan Chuan-Jin | NUS Medicine profile, he is a former Speaker of Parliament of Singapore, Cabinet Minister, President of the Singapore National Olympic Council and Brigadier-General in the Singapore Armed Forces.

Military Leadership

Tan Chuan-Jin served in the Singapore Armed Forces and reached the rank of Brigadier-General.

One of the significant assignments highlighted by NUS was his command of Operation Flying Eagle. The operation was Singapore's largest overseas military deployment during the 2004 Indian Ocean tsunami.

The experience is relevant when considering leadership during emergencies, where organisations must coordinate people and resources while responding to rapidly changing circumstances.

Public Service and Government

Tan Chuan-Jin subsequently held senior government positions and worked across several ministries.

His government responsibilities included work connected with the Ministry of Manpower, Ministry of National Development, and Ministry of Social and Family Development.

According to NUS, his work covered areas including employment practices, housing, early childhood development, social safety nets, political and community engagement, environmental sustainability, sports development and social causes.

This breadth of experience illustrates how public-sector leadership can involve interconnected policy areas rather than a single specialised field.

Parliamentary Leadership

Tan Chuan-Jin also served as Speaker of the Parliament of Singapore.

The role of a parliamentary speaker involves responsibilities connected with the conduct of parliamentary proceedings and the functioning of the legislature. His parliamentary experience forms another part of a career that has moved between operational, governmental and institutional leadership.

Leadership, Strategy and Systems

His professional work has included entrepreneurship, payments solutions, consultancy and advisory activities focused on leadership, strategy and systems.

NUS also describes his interest in mentoring leaders across sectors and his focus on systems thinking, stewardship and organisational culture.

These themes are relevant to organisations dealing with complex problems because effective leadership often requires looking beyond individual decisions and considering how people, processes and institutions interact.

Social Impact and Giving

Another area highlighted in the NUS profile is Tan Chuan-Jin's interest in volunteering and giving.

His work focuses on finding ways to make volunteering and giving part of everyday life, based on the idea that a stronger culture of giving can contribute to broader societal impact.

He also advocates a "We before Me" approach to leadership, alongside purposeful stewardship and the development of organisational cultures that support resilience, adaptability and service.

A Cross-Sector Leadership Career

Tan Chuan-Jin's career spans several environments with very different responsibilities. His military service involved operational leadership, his government career involved public policy and administration, and his later work has included organisational strategy, entrepreneurship and social initiatives.

The combination provides an interesting example of how leadership experience can be transferred between sectors.
